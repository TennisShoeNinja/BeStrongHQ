# Code of Conduct

## The Short Version

BeStrongHQ exists to help coaches and athletes get stronger together. That only works if this community feels the same way: welcoming, honest, and grown-up about disagreements. Be the kind of person you'd want on your team.

## Our Pledge

We, maintainers, contributors, coaches, athletes, and everyone in between, pledge to make participation in the BeStrongHQ community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act in ways that make this an open, welcoming, and genuinely useful community for people trying to do good work in strength coaching and software.

## Our Standards

Things that make this community better:

- Treating people with empathy and respect, especially when you disagree
- Giving clear, constructive feedback, and taking it the same way
- Owning mistakes, apologizing where it matters, and moving forward
- Assuming good faith when reading someone else's code, comment, or training philosophy
- Putting the community's long-term health above your short-term point

Things that don't belong here:

- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted advances of any kind
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, or personal and political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing anyone's private information (address, email, phone, gym affiliation) without their clear permission
- Anything else you'd get pulled aside for in a professional setting

## BeStrongHQ-Specific Expectations

Because this project sits at the intersection of coaching and software, a few extra ground rules:

- **Respect different coaching philosophies.** Powerlifting, Olympic lifting, general strength and conditioning, hybrid: people come here from different backgrounds. Debate methodologies on the merits. Don't dismiss someone's approach as "wrong" because it isn't yours.
- **Athlete data is sacred.** If you're working with real athlete data (spreadsheets, screenshots, exported reports), scrub names, contact info, and anything identifying before posting it in an issue, PR, or discussion. When in doubt, ask a maintainer first.
- **No prescriptive medical or training advice.** Share experience, cite sources, describe what worked for your athletes. Don't issue programming or medical guidance as if this repo were your coaching platform. The software is the product; the coaching stays with the coach.
- **Code review is about the code.** Critique the approach, not the person who wrote it. "This won't scale because X" lands; "why would you write it this way" doesn't.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and will take appropriate, fair action in response to behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that aren't aligned with this Code of Conduct, and will explain moderation decisions when it's appropriate to do so.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all BeStrongHQ community spaces (the repo, issues, PRs, discussions), and also when someone is officially representing the project in public spaces (official email, official social media, speaking at an event as a BeStrongHQ rep).

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior to the maintainers at **alex@bestronghq.com**. All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

Maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of anyone reporting an incident.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers will follow these Community Impact Guidelines when deciding consequences:

### 1. Correction
**Impact:** Inappropriate language or behavior considered unprofessional or unwelcome.
**Consequence:** A private, written warning explaining what was wrong and why. A public apology may be requested.

### 2. Warning
**Impact:** A violation through a single incident or series of actions.
**Consequence:** A warning with consequences for continued behavior. No interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited contact with those enforcing the Code of Conduct, for a specified period. Applies in community spaces and external channels like social media. Continued violations may lead to a temporary or permanent ban.

### 3. Temporary Ban
**Impact:** A serious violation, including sustained inappropriate behavior.
**Consequence:** A temporary ban from any interaction or public communication with the community for a specified period. No public or private interaction with the people involved is allowed during this period. Violating these terms may lead to a permanent ban.

### 4. Permanent Ban
**Impact:** A pattern of violations, sustained inappropriate behavior, harassment, or aggression toward or disparagement of groups of people.
**Consequence:** A permanent ban from any public interaction within the community.
